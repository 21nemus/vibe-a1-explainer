How to use

  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan.
  • 1–4 follow power, data or balance. W whole, C cutaway, E exploded.
  • Hover a part to name it. Click a servo to look inside it.
  • Space plays the guided tour. S switches scene. ↑ ↓ change the speed.

Sources

Geometry, joint axes, limits, masses and the recorded wave come from the Vibe Robotics SDK (Apache-2.0, MuJoCo models exported from Vibe's Onshape CAD). Specs: viberobotics.co/docs. Servo data: Feetech STS3215 / STS3250 datasheets. The internal electronics layout is illustrative; the parts come from Vibe's bill of materials.
